Every golf season, Princeton Golf Club hosts 4 to 5 golf tournaments attracting golf enthusiasts from New Jersey and surrounding areas to meet and compete. This is a place where you can make new friends and enjoy a great game of golf. Princeton Golf Club started in 2008 and has since hosted many successful seasons of golf tournament.
