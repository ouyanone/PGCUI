Game Rules

Princeton Golf Club — Official Tournament Rules

1

Team Formation

Depending on the number of players, all participants are divided into two teams: Red Team and Blue Team.
2

Match Play Format

On each tee, players from each team play against each other. Normally two players from the Red Team compete against two players from the Blue Team.
3

High-Low Scoring

Each hole score uses the high-low rule: the best score of the Red Team is compared against the best score of the Blue Team.
4

Hole Points

Winning a hole earns 1 point. If one team's lower score also beats the other team's best score, an additional point is awarded.
★

Bonus Points

Birdie +1 pt
Eagle +5 pts
Hole-in-One +10 pts
5

Final Result

After the game, all points from each team are totalled. The team with the highest score wins the tournament.
