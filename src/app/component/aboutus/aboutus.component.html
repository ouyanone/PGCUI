新泽西普林斯顿高尔夫球俱乐部

Est. 2008 · New Jersey

auto_stories Our Story 我们的故事

Founded in 2008, the New Jersey Princeton Golf Club has brought together golf enthusiasts from across the tri-state area for over 15 years. What started as a small gathering of passionate golfers has grown into a vibrant community united by a love of the game and a spirit of friendly competition.

自 2008 年成立以来，普林斯顿高尔夫球俱乐部汇聚了来自新泽西州及周边地区的高尔夫爱好者。 十多年来，我们从一个小型聚会成长为一个充满活力的社区，以球会友，以赛促情。

President, PGC Golf Club · Since 2025

Since taking the helm as President in 2025, Alan Long has led the New Jersey Princeton Golf Club to a highly successful season — strengthening the club's competitive spirit, growing membership, and fostering the warm community that defines PGC. Under his leadership, the club looks forward to even greater achievements in the seasons ahead.
